## Suggested implementation order

The first implementation block should combine scheduled publishing, draft-from-letter, source-quality labels, revision history, and the publication checklist. The second should deepen discovery with map legend/filtering, timeline cards, and related-case graph. The final block should focus on private reader utilities and the quieter field-station details so the site remains a research journal rather than a noisy social product.
