## Experience Principles Adopted

The journal will preserve a focused “quiet room” reader while adding author-side discovery structures, rather than adding generic dashboard clutter. Useful principles from the long-form and information-design sources include a visible read time, descriptive captions and source links, strong typography and whitespace, deliberate visual interruptions between text sections, limited navigation routes, clear evidence labels, and information architecture that helps readers understand context as well as find content. The public experience will therefore use a compact category system, source cards, a related-record trail, reading preferences, and image caption/reference fields. The admin experience will use one structured record editor rather than unrelated widgets.
